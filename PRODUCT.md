# PRODUCT.md — apeLabs

Product truth for design work on apelabs.dev. Visual decisions live in DESIGN.md.
Sources: positioning (locked 2026-10-27), the approved site copy deck (2026-10-30), the site direction brief.

## What it is

apeLabs is a small studio that fixes and builds the business systems small teams run on. It speaks as "we";
the founder stays in the background (no name, photo or bio on the site). Brand spelling: apeLabs.

## Who it is for

Small service businesses of about 5 to 50 people, in the US, working remotely with the studio. Their
automations and integrations (Zapier, Make, n8n, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Airtable, Google Sheets,
QuickBooks, payment tools, custom code) stop working without an error, and they find out when a customer goes
cold or a record goes missing. Individuals can ask; there is no section for them.

## The offer

- Front door: a **free diagnostic**, a free, bounded look at one problem (no numeric cap is stated). The visitor
  gets a short written note on what's broken and one fixed quote.
- **Repair** (main): fixed quote before work, repairs start at $150, an acceptance check agreed in writing, no
  charge if the repair fails it, 14-day warranty on the same issue. Care plan $120/mo after a paid repair.
- Menu behind it: AI systems setup from $300 (local/private AI included, the client buys hardware directly),
  second opinion $250, AI workshop $400, custom builds by quote.
- AI is a capability ("we build with AI where it helps"), never the label.

## What the visitor must do

Request a free diagnostic through one asynchronous form (no phone field, no budget field, no call needed).
A person reads every request and replies within one business day. Calls happen on Google Meet after the request.

## Proof that exists

No client repair records yet, and the site says so. It shows a labelled fictional sample finding (Harbor Plumbing
Co.) and two own builds (sur9e, hermes-finance), with no usage numbers. No logos, testimonials, metrics, people or
repo links until cleared.

## Constraints

- Copy is the approved deck, verbatim. Never invent claims, metrics, clients or prices.
- No passwords are ever requested. Form data is kept 90 days.
- Analytics without cookies (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Spam check: Cloudflare Turnstile.
- Every sample or illustrative artefact is labelled as such where a visitor could mistake it for real.
- Must work without JavaScript and without motion; accessible by keyboard and screen reader.

## Mode

Persuade (landing and service pages), with the FAQ and privacy pages read as Read surfaces.
